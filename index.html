Our Story

Art born where the land meets the sea

Seashell is an Azerbaijani atelier turning the textures of the coast into lasting art. We gather shells, pour resin like still water and compose each piece by hand — no two are ever alike.

From a small studio in Baku, our work now travels to homes around the world — carrying a little of the Caspian with it.
